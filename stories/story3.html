Trailer Trouble

Written by: Reim

This is not my own story but one from my mother. When she was 17 years old, she went to the club together with her sister. Inside the club her sister made a rude comment about the clothes of another girl. This comment was overheard by the girl who got angry and for the rest of the night she would step on the heels of my mother and her sister. When my mother and her sister tried to leave the club around 5 or 6 cars were waiting for them together with the girl. It turned out the girl was from a trailer park and she called all her friends. This scared my mother and her sister a lot, so they decided to call the bouncers who were able to delay the people long enough for my mother and her sister to get away. Initially they were planning to file a police report, but decided not to, because they would have to disclose their names which could be used to find out where they lived. After this incident my mother got scared, she would run into the girl. Luckily this kind of thing does not happen often anymore, but it is still something to be mindful of.
