A fall in the High Tatras

Written by: Alex

My bad experience happened while I was mountain climbing in Slovakia’s High Tatras, above a mountain hut called Téryho chata. During the climb, I fell about twenty metres off a cliff. That is a very different kind of travel story from visiting a city or looking around a historic square. The place was part of the same country I enjoyed exploring, but this experience involved real danger. A fall of that distance is a serious accident, even in a landscape people usually associate with beautiful views. This is the bad part of my story because it shows another side of being in the mountains. They can be impressive and beautiful, but they also deserve respect. My experience gives a very personal meaning to that idea, till this day I feel very lucky I survived that experience with relatively minor injuries and was able to make mostly a full recovery.
