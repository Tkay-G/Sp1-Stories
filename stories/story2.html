Water guns & Rifles

Written by: Daniel

This is a day I wish had never happened, I and my family went to visit our grandpa during the holiday, it was Christmas at that time. After relaxing and all. I, my sister and one my cousin went up a hill ,a very rough one actually, there was a building on top made of mud. We felt it was abandoned already, To our amusement people where living there, it was supposed to be a fun interaction with them, but they weren't so calm because my cousin was holdg a water gun to their faces. I think they felt threaten, one of the kids just went inside a brought out a rifle for hunting bush animals, their father was probably an hunter and he wasn't around so they had access to it. These kids literally loaded the rifle and decide they were going to fire in 5 seconds, if we didn,t go down. We didn't hesitate at all we ran faster than the speed of light and fear in our mind, since that day I never joked around villagers kid's.
