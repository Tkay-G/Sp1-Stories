Water guns & Rifles

Written by: Daniel

This is a day I wish had never happened, I and my family went to visit our grandpa during the holiday, it was Christmas at that time.
After relaxing, my sister, a cousin and I, went up a hill; it was quite rough and at the top there was a building made of mud.
We thought it was abandoned, however to our amusement other people, children, where living there.
The interaction we had, was meant to be fun, but they weren't so calm because my cousin was holding a water gun to their faces.
They most likely felt threatened, and just like that one of the kids just went inside and brought out a rifle.
It looked like a hunting rifle meaning their father was probably a hunter, and unfortunately for us, he wasn't around.
These kids loaded the rifle and decide they were going to shoot us in 5 seconds, if we didn’t leave.
We didn't hesitate at all, we ran faster than the speed of light and with the fear in our mind as our fuel. Since that day I never joked around with villager's kids.
